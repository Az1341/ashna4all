📋 All 48 Squads Confirmed
🌍 FIFA Official
2 June 2026
All 48 World Cup squads officially confirmed by FIFA
All 48 nations submitted their final 26-player squads to FIFA by 1 June. Each team names up to 26 players including three goalkeepers. Players can still be replaced due to serious injury no later than 24 hours before their team's first match. Over 1,248 players are heading to North America — the most in World Cup history.
Full squad lists on ESPN ↗
🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿 England
England — 26-Man Squad · Group L
Group L · Croatia · Ghana · Panama · Manager: Thomas Tuchel

Harry Kane leads England as captain. Notable absentees include Phil Foden and Cole Palmer (both below-par seasons). Ivan Toney earns a surprise recall. Eberechi Eze and Noni Madueke included. England open vs Croatia on 17 June in Dallas (21:00 BST).
Key players: Kane · Pickford · Bellingham · Saka · Toney ✅ · Eze ✅ · Foden ❌ · Palmer ❌
