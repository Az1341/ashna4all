📋 All 48 Squads Confirmed
🌍 FIFA Official
2 June 2026
All 48 World Cup squads officially confirmed by FIFA
All 48 nations submitted their final 26-player squads to FIFA by 1 June, with official confirmation on 2 June. Each team names up to 26 players including three goalkeepers. Players can still be replaced due to serious injury or illness, no later than 24 hours before their team's first match. Over 1,248 players are heading to North America — the most in World Cup history.

England — 26-Man Squad · Group L
Group L · Croatia · Ghana · Panama · Manager: Thomas Tuchel

Harry Kane leads England as captain. Notable absentees include Phil Foden and Cole Palmer, both of whom had below-par club seasons. Ivan Toney earns a surprise recall. Eberechi Eze and Noni Madueke are included, as is Tino Livramento despite a recent thigh injury. England open against Croatia on 17 June in Dallas (21:00 BST).

🌍 FIFA Rule Change
All 26 players at centre circle for national anthems
FIFA have announced that all players in the matchday squad — not just the starting eleven — will gather at the centre circle for national anthems. Every squad member gets that moment of representing their country on the world stage.
Read on FIFA.com ↗
🇧🇷 Brazil
Brazil turn to 34-year-old Neymar after Rodrygo & Estêvão injuries
With both Rodrygo and Estêvão ruled out through injury, Brazil have recalled veteran forward Neymar to provide attacking width. Brazil open their campaign against Morocco on 13 June in East Rutherford, New Jersey.
Read on ESPN ↗
🇮🇹 Italy Missing
Italy absent for the third consecutive World Cup
Four-time world champions Italy have failed to qualify for the third World Cup in a row, losing a play-off final on penalties. Stars such as Donnarumma and Sandro Tonali will be watching from home while the tournament takes place in North America.
