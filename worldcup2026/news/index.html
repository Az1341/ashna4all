📋 All 48 Squads Confirmed
🌍 FIFA Official · 2 June 2026
All 48 World Cup squads officially confirmed by FIFA
All 48 nations submitted their final 26-player squads to FIFA by 1 June, with official confirmation on 2 June. Each team can name up to 26 players including three goalkeepers. Players can still be replaced from the provisional squad in the event of serious injury or illness, no later than 24 hours before their team's first match. Over 1,000 players are heading to North America — the most in World Cup history due to the expanded 48-team format.
