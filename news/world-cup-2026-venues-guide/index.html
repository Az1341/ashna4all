World Cup 2026 Venues — Guide to All 16 Stadiums

📅 2026-06-03 ✍️ Ahmad Zafarani ⚽ GoalCurrent.live

16 Iconic Venues Across Three Nations

The 2026 FIFA World Cup will be played across 16 stunning stadiums in the United States, Canada, and Mexico. From iconic NFL venues converted for football to purpose-built soccer stadiums, the facilities on offer are truly world-class.

United States Venues

The USA hosts the majority of matches, with venues in New York/New Jersey, Los Angeles, Dallas, San Francisco, Seattle, Miami, Boston, Philadelphia, Kansas City, Atlanta, and Houston. MetLife Stadium in New Jersey will host the Grand Final on 19 July.

Canadian Venues

Canada hosts matches in Toronto and Vancouver. Both cities are passionate about football and will provide an excellent atmosphere for the group stage matches taking place there.

Mexican Venues

Mexico City's iconic Estadio Azteca is one of the most famous football venues in the world. Guadalajara and Monterrey complete the Mexican trio of host cities, with passionate local fans expected to create unforgettable atmospheres.

Attending a Match

If you are planning to attend a World Cup 2026 match, check the official FIFA ticketing portal for availability. Match tickets can be combined with transport and accommodation packages, and planning well in advance is highly recommended.

Fan Zones

Each host city will have official FIFA Fan Zones where supporters without match tickets can watch games on big screens and enjoy the World Cup atmosphere. These are free to enter and family-friendly environments perfect for all fans.
