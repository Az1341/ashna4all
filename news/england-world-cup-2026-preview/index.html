England at World Cup 2026 — Can the Lions Finally Win It?

📅 2026-06-05 ✍️ Ahmad Zafarani ⚽ GoalCurrent.live

Sixty Years of Hurt — Can It End in 2026?

England haven't won the World Cup since 1966, and the 2026 tournament represents another golden opportunity. With a talented squad featuring Premier League stars from across the top clubs, expectations are high going into the tournament.

Squad Analysis

The England squad is arguably one of the most talented in a generation. Blessed with quality in every position, the manager has plenty of options when it comes to team selection and tactical flexibility.

Key Players to Watch

England have match-winners throughout the squad. Their attack is capable of causing any defence in the world problems, and the defensive unit has been solid throughout the qualification campaign.

Tactical Setup

The manager is expected to set England up in a structured shape that is hard to beat, while having the attacking quality to unlock defences. The balance between solidity and creativity will be key to England's success.

Group Stage Fixtures

England face a tough group but should have enough quality to progress. The matches against France will be the headline fixture that fans around the world will be watching. Victory in the group would set up a more favourable knockout path.

Prediction

England have the quality to go all the way, but consistency and avoiding injuries will be crucial. A semi-final berth looks realistic, with the final not out of the question if everything clicks at the right time.
