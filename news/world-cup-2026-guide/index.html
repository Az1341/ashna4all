FIFA World Cup 2026 — The Complete Fan Guide

📅 2026-06-01 ✍️ Ahmad Zafarani ⚽ GoalCurrent.live

The Biggest World Cup Ever

The 2026 FIFA World Cup is the largest in the tournament's history, expanding to 48 teams for the first time. Hosted jointly by the United States, Canada, and Mexico, the tournament features 104 matches across 16 world-class venues from 11 June to 19 July 2026.

Format & Groups

For the first time, 12 groups of 4 teams each will compete in the group stage. The top two from each group and the eight best third-placed sides advance to a 32-team knockout round. This creates more matches, more drama, and more opportunities for smaller nations to shine on the global stage.

Host Cities & Venues

Matches are spread across iconic stadiums in cities including New York/New Jersey, Los Angeles, Dallas, San Francisco, Seattle, Miami, Boston, Philadelphia, Kansas City, Atlanta, Houston, Guadalajara, Mexico City, Monterrey, Toronto, and Vancouver.

Teams to Watch

France enter as one of the favourites, boasting a squad packed with world-class talent. Brazil are always dangerous, as are Argentina — the reigning champions seeking back-to-back titles. England, Spain, and Germany will also be strong contenders from Europe.

Key Dates

The group stage runs from 11 June to 2 July. The round of 32 begins 4 July, with the quarter-finals on 18-19 July, semi-finals on 14-15 July, and the Grand Final on 19 July 2026 at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey.
