World Cup 2026 — All 12 Group Stage Previews

📅 2026-06-10 ✍️ Ahmad Zafarani ⚽ GoalCurrent.live

Group A — The Host Group

Mexico, South Africa, Korea Republic, and Czechia make up Group A. Mexico, as one of the co-hosts, will be roared on by passionate home support. South Africa make their return to a World Cup and Korea Republic always threaten to produce upsets on the biggest stage.

Group B — South American Power

Argentina defend their title in Group B alongside Canada, Chile, and Peru. The reigning champions are strong favourites to progress, but the South American rivalry adds extra spice to every fixture in this group.

Group C — CONCACAF Home Advantage

The USA play in Group C with Morocco, Panama, and Senegal. Playing on home soil gives the USMNT a significant boost, and with a talented young squad, they will aim to go deep into the tournament.

Group D — The Group of Death

Spain, Brazil, Japan, and Cameroon form what many are calling the toughest group. Spain and Brazil are both genuine title contenders, making this a must-watch group from the very first match.

Groups E through L

The remaining groups feature all the major European powers including France, England, Germany, Italy, Portugal, Belgium, Netherlands, Croatia, Denmark, and Serbia. Each group has its own storylines, rivalries, and potential upsets waiting to happen.

Our Predictions

We expect the traditional powers to dominate, but the expanded format means more opportunity for surprise packages. Keep an eye on Morocco, Japan, and South Korea as potential quarter-final dark horses.
