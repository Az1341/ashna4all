🏆 PSG · Champions
📅 30 May 2026 · Budapest
PSG retain Champions League — Back-to-Back glory in Budapest penalty drama
Paris Saint-Germain became only the second club to retain the UEFA Champions League title since Real Madrid in 2016–17, beating Arsenal 4–3 on penalties after a tense 1–1 draw after extra time at the Puskás Aréna. Kai Havertz gave Arsenal a dream start after just six minutes, but Ousmane Dembélé's penalty on 65 minutes levelled the tie. Despite 120 minutes of intense football, neither side could find a winner — until the shootout, where Gabriel's penalty sailed over the bar to hand PSG the trophy. Vitinha was named Man of the Match.

🔴 Arsenal · Reaction
📅 30 May 2026 · Post-Match
Arsenal heartbreak — Defeat on penalties ends incredible European journey
Arsenal's remarkable 15-game unbeaten Champions League campaign ended in the cruellest way — penalties. Having gone 20 years without reaching a UCL Final, Mikel Arteta's side gave everything. They led through Havertz, defended brilliantly for long periods, and David Raya even saved Nuno Mendes' penalty to keep the shootout alive. But Eze and Gabriel both missed, and PSG retained the trophy. Sky Sports described it as "progress but a missed opportunity" for Arsenal. Declan Rice said after the final: "Defeat won't define us."
