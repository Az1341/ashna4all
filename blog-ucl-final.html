🏆 2025/26 UEFA Champions League Final

🔍 Match Overview

The biggest night in European club football arrives on Saturday 30 May 2026 as Paris Saint-Germain face Arsenal in the UEFA Champions League Final at the spectacular Puskás Aréna in Budapest, Hungary.

This is a truly historic final. PSG arrive as defending champions, having claimed their first ever UCL title in 2025. Arsenal, fresh from winning the Premier League 2025/26 title, are appearing in their first Champions League final since their defeat in 2006 — and they are desperate to finally claim the trophy that has eluded them for 20 years.

"This is the biggest night in Arsenal's recent history. Premier League champions AND European champions in the same season would be one of the greatest achievements in the club's history."

🇫🇷 Paris Saint-Germain

PSG are bidding to become only the second club in the Champions League era to successfully defend their title, after Real Madrid won three in a row between 2016 and 2018. Under their French coach, PSG have been devastating in Europe this season — fast, fluid and lethal on the counter-attack.

Key players to watch:

⭐ Ousmane Dembele ⭐ Bradley Barcola ⭐ Vitinha ⭐ Achraf Hakimi ⭐ Gianluigi Donnarumma

🔴 Arsenal

Arsenal arrive as Premier League 2025/26 champions and in the finest form in the club's recent history. Mikel Arteta has built a team of incredible quality, depth and spirit. For Arsenal, this is their chance to complete a domestic and European double that would cement this squad as one of the greatest in their history.

Key players to watch:

⭐ Bukayo Saka ⭐ Martin Odegaard ⭐ Declan Rice ⭐ Kai Havertz ⭐ Gabriel Martinelli
