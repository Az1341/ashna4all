🔴 Arsenal FC · 31 May 2026
🏆 Arsenal's Champions Parade takes over the streets of Islington
Thousands of Arsenal fans lined the streets of North London on Sunday 31 May 2026 as the Gunners celebrated their Premier League title with an open-top bus parade through Islington. The parade started at 2pm and featured four open-top buses carrying both the men's and women's teams. The men's squad celebrated the Premier League title, while the women's team showed off the FIFA Women's Champions Cup. A Champions truck with DJs led the procession. Arsenal won the Premier League 2025/26 with 85 points — their first title since 2003/04.
